## [1.0.0] - 2026-03-23

### Initial Public Release

First public release of the Mars Multi-Mission Architecture concept.

#### Core Architecture
- 10-mission framework (Ares I through X)
- Natural redundancy through mission cadence ("your backup lands with you")
- Pre-position and verify model
- Mars Ops from Day One (crews oversee each other's critical phases)
- MTRV chain (each transit vehicle becomes next mission's return vehicle)
- Two Dragons per mission (Mars Dragon + Cargo Dragon)
- HAB-by-HAB base growth (Core, Living, Lab, Farm, Shop, Home)
- ISRU-based propellant production
- Cycler initiation at Ares VIII

#### Infrastructure
- Base Alpha (6 HABs by Ares V)
- Base Beta (established Ares VII)
- Earth Departure Station (Ares VI+)
- Mars Bus Stop (Ares VIII+)
- Propellant depots (Earth and Mars orbit)

#### Partnership Model
- Four major partners: NASA, ESA, JAXA, CSA
- Two minor partners: ISRO, UAESA
- Open partnership framework for future entrants
- Domain assignments by heritage
- Crew slot allocation model

#### Cost Analysis
- Total program: $69-117B over 25 years
- Phase-gated funding strategy
- Per-mission breakdowns

#### Communications
- 5-part video series structure
- Partnership through-line ("Apollo was American. Mars will be human.")
- Medium post hook

#### Documentation
- Full architecture briefing (~1,600 lines)
- Cost and partnership analysis
- Risks, gaps, and honest limitations
- This changelog
